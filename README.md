# Glimmers of Elsewhere · 异境拾光

**➡️ Want to turn one sentence into your own playable little world? Go here first: [GlimmersElsewhereCreate · 一句话生成世界工具](https://github.com/glimmerselsewhere/GlimmersElsewhereCreate)** — a standalone creator you run locally with your own model (no keys included).

---

This repository is the playable site of **Glimmers of Elsewhere** — a small shelf of cat-sized worlds, all played by cats.

- 🌐 Play online: **https://glimmerselsewhere.github.io/GlimmersElsewhere/?lang=en** （中文：`?lang=zh`）
- 🐈 Flagship world — *Little Grey on the Street* (a five-year life of a stray cat, six endings, each ending comes with a 3D-printable figurine): **https://glimmerselsewhere.github.io/GlimmersElsewhere/worlds/stray-cat/?lang=en**
- 🛠️ One-sentence world creator (standalone, open source): **https://github.com/glimmerselsewhere/GlimmersElsewhereCreate**

## What's inside

- A tiny web engine (HTML/CSS/JS + Three.js) that plays small, single-level story games — every world opens with its own cover art and is played from cat height.
- Worlds of different flavours: a first-person cat patrol, a cultivation isekai, a kitchen that cooks for elves and dragons, an image-guessing stage — and *Little Grey on the Street*, a quiet, honest five-year stray-cat life.
- Ending keepsakes: each ending can hand you a figurine, generated with **Tripo P2** as a downloadable GLB you can spin in the in-game 3D showroom.
- Bilingual: English / 中文, with `?lang=en|zh` on every link.
